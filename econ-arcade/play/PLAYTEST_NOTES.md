# Econ Arcade living-world playtest

This playtest intentionally hides the curriculum from the player.

## Player-facing loop

1. Enter a place with characters and stakes.
2. Hear a briefing or conversation.
3. Predict what will happen and explain why.
4. Make a consequential economic decision.
5. Experience a narrated outcome from deterministic game logic.
6. Answer Osiris's targeted question about the mechanism.
7. Discover the economic concept only after reasoning through it.
8. Return to Meridian, where Osiris chooses the next thread from the hidden mastery model.

## Design rule

**Never teach economics when the player can discover economics.**

The visible experience is story, characters, choices, consequences, and discovery. Mastery scores remain hidden and are used only by Osiris to choose future situations.

## Current worlds

- **Market Row / Lemon Empire** — pricing, demand, input shocks, unit economics, marginal thinking, opportunity cost.
- **The Heights / The Mandate** — policy lags, expectations, credibility, inflation/growth tradeoffs, market repricing.
- **Low Harbor / Terms of Trust** — reputation, reciprocity, signaling, repeated games, cooperation and betrayal.

The browser playtest uses deterministic local logic and device-local persistence. The dedicated `Ballzatram/osairis` backend remains the production path for richer AI dialogue and server-side player models.
