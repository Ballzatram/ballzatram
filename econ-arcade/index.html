A curriculum that adapts to the player

Econ Arcade uses a repeatable teaching loop: make a choice under pressure, reveal the hidden incentives and consequences, then let Osiris update a shared model of your economic reasoning. The next game is selected because of what you need to experience next—not because a syllabus says so.
