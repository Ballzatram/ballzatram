Plain-English privacy

Privacy

Ballzatram is experimental. Some tools are prototypes, some may change, and AI outputs may be wrong.

Local prototype data

Ballzatram may use localStorage for prototype favorites, votes, and suggestions. This data is saved in your browser for now and is not a paid member account system.

Your AI connections

AI is optional. The local preview makes no model calls. The chat-app option prepares text for you to copy; you decide whether to paste and send it in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.

With OpenRouter, your selected question and context go directly from your browser to OpenRouter and the model provider using your own account. A direct API connection sends that data and your API key through the relay you choose. Use only a relay you operate or trust. The provider’s privacy, retention, and billing rules apply.

Connection preferences are stored locally. API keys use this tab’s session storage for up to four hours, or page memory if storage is blocked. Session restoration may keep tab storage until expiry. Scripts on this site’s origin can access tab storage. Disconnect removes the key from this tab; revoke it in your provider account to disable it everywhere. Ballzatram’s AI workspace does not send keys to an account database.

Questions prepared in another tool stay in tab storage for up to one hour. Only selected context is transferred. Review the context before sending. Chat-app responses do not sync back automatically.

Uploads and media

Users are responsible for uploaded media rights. Media tools require owned, licensed, or permission-approved content.

If uploads are used in AI Edit, server-side storage and retention must be governed by the deployed AI Edit policy.

Advice boundaries

Ballzatram does not provide financial, legal, trading, real estate, or investment guidance. Paper-mode tools are educational simulations only.

Payments

Checkout is not live yet. Paid privacy terms will be updated before payments are accepted.

Contact

Questions can go to hello@ballzatram.com unless a better project contact is published later.
