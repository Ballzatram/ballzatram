The project has a context adapter and instructions in the code. To receive AI answers, the separate Osiris service must be deployed and you must connect your own ChatGPT account. This page does not verify a live provider connection.

The current assistant explains selected information. It does not change game state, trade, edit records, publish findings, render media, or import your chat history. Each of those workflows needs its own deliberate integration.
