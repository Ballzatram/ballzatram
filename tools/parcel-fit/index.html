A PRACTICAL PROCESS

From listing to informed next step.

Starting small on purpose: this is an analyst-reviewed research service, not automated site certification.

01

Tell us the actual plan.

Location, intended use, minimum usable area, facilities, and whatever property listing or parcel ID you already have.

02

We check the evidence.

Desktop review of available parcel, floodplain, wetland, soils, terrain, zoning, and access information—where records permit.

03

Get a clear research memo.

Sources, potential deal-breakers, unanswered questions, and which licensed local professionals should verify them next.

ⓘ

Evidence matters. Missing source data stays “unknown,” not “safe.” A desk screen never substitutes for survey, engineering, title work, local zoning confirmation, or legal advice.

THE FINE PRINT

Good questions, answered.

Does Parcel Fit tell me if land is approved for my project?

No. This is a preliminary desk screen intended to surface risk and due-diligence questions. Local zoning offices, licensed surveyors, engineers, environmental professionals, and legal counsel make the relevant determinations.

Will the report use AI?

AI may assist with research organization and drafting, but factual claims must be tied to reviewed evidence. Unknowns remain unknown. The founding pilots are reviewed by a human before delivery.

Do I need a ChatGPT or Claude subscription?

No. This is a service, not a subscription-to-AI integration. You share a property brief and receive the scoped research deliverable.

How do payment and delivery work?

Send the inquiry first. You'll receive a scoped offer with total price, expected timing, and coverage before accepting or paying. Online checkout is not currently offered on this page.
